A new chapter: I'm building MySlotMate as its Founder & CEO. ✦

MySlotMate is a marketplace for real-world experiences. You can book things to do near you, like a pottery night, a sunrise trek or a supper club, or host your own. The website is live today, and the iOS and Android apps are coming soon.

Here's how it came to be.

It started with a conversation. Subrata pitched me an idea: one place to find real things to do, with real people, near you. That idea stayed with me, and it's the spark behind everything that followed.

I started with design. I built the brand from scratch, shaped the product vision, and went through more iterations than I can count until the idea felt real.

Then Saipan joined the vision, taking on the financials, the pitch deck, marketing and so much of the ideation that turned a design into a plan.

Finally, I took the designs I had created and pushed myself from designer into full front-end developer, screen by screen, until the website went live. The app is next.

We're building MySlotMate from Northeast India, and we're going all in.

Subrata, thank you for the spark. Saipan, thank you for believing early and carrying so much of the load.

Less meetings. More moments.
