Website architecture · story-first

From a product you use to a story you show up for.

A page-by-page flow for the MySlotMate website. It borrows how Offlyn joins marketing and product into one site, and it tells MySlotMate's own story: people's time, booked and spent well.

Hero idea: a night skyline. Every lit window is a slot happening in the city right now.

01 · What Offlyn gets right

Four pages, each with one job

Offlyn feels like more than a product because each page does a different job. The homepage hooks you with a feeling and then gets straight to things to do. Every other page sells one audience at a time.

How Offlyn is built

  • Home: a surreal one-line hero ("Because life happens Offlyn"), then the live events grid with a city picker, a "Didn't find anything? Host it" band, and host guides.
  • About: a manifesto that runs Why now → What we're building → Why the founders built it → Who it's for → What we stand for → a closing line.
  • For Hosts: a long sales page. Animated counters, a marquee of event formats, mock product cards labelled "Example", the money breakdown, a comparison table and an FAQ.
  • For Brands: a business page laid out as a four-step process with ROI proof.
  • Throughout: city SEO pages, a blog, a floating "Ask Offlyn" assistant and app download links.

What MySlotMate does differently

  • The story is on the homepage. Offlyn keeps its story on About. MySlotMate tells the brand story as you scroll, then shows the product as the answer to it.
  • Time is the motif. The site is told across one weekend: Friday's scroll, Saturday's slot, Sunday's memory. It fits the tagline "Book People's Time".
  • Night, not daylight. Offlyn uses a bright meadow. MySlotMate keeps its dark navy and violet, with the city lit up at night.
  • Two kinds of host. Community hosts and professionals get separate pages, because what they need to hear is different.
  • The Journey. Offlyn has nothing like the app's memory timeline. It's MySlotMate's emotional payoff, so it gets its own chapter.

03 · Homepage

One weekend, ten chapters

Every chapter is labelled with a time, so the homepage reads like a weekend. One slot pill follows the reader down the page. It starts as something they see, gets booked, and ends up as a memory.

Act 1 · Friday night the problem
Act 2 · Saturday the answer, the product
Act 3 · Sunday what it leaves you with
Ch 01Fri · 6:40 PM

Hero: the city, lit up

Job: make someone feel the brand in one second and give them a way in. The visual is a night skyline where windows light up one by one. Hovering a window shows the slot behind it.

Headline

Life happens when you show up.

Sub: Book People's Time.

Actions

Primary: Find something to do. Secondary: Share your time. Under the CTAs: a strip of three slots happening tonight in your city.

Reuse

The gradient pill button and the location chip ("Guwahati") from the app.

Ch 02Fri · 9:15 PM

The scroll: name the problem

Job: say the thing everyone feels. A phone feed scrolls on its own and slows as the copy builds. Then the screen goes dark.

Copy, line by line

We scroll. We follow. We save it for another day.

Then: Discovering something online isn't the same as living it.

Source

From the brand story: "connecting with people has never been easier, yet meaningful connections often feel harder to find."

Ch 03Sat · 9:00 AM

The people: everyone has something worth sharing

Job: move the story from "things to do" to "people to meet", which is MySlotMate's real difference. Four portrait cards. On hover or tap, each one flips to show the slot that person offers.

The four people

A local artist who wants to teach. A traveller who knows the hidden trails. A creator who brings people together. A professional with years of knowledge.

Card back

"Sat · 10 AM · Pottery basics · ₹800" or "Free community walk". This shows that both free and paid slots exist.

Reuse

The Host card and Role Label (Community Host / Professional Host) components.

Ch 05Sat · 11:30 AM

Ch 06Sat · 4:00 PM

Communities: where it keeps going

Job: show that MySlotMate isn't one-off ticketing. Bring in the community feed from the app: an announcement post, a live poll, a photo recap.

Headline

Shared interests become shared experiences.

Reuse

Ch 07Sat · 7:00 PM

The moment: you showed up

Job: the emotional peak. One full-width, warm, real photo of a room of people and almost no interface. The only UI is a quiet check-in toast.

Copy

Walk in as a guest. Leave knowing someone.

Why

The warm photos against the dark interface are already your best visual contrast. This chapter makes full use of it.

Ch 08Sun · 10:00 AM

Your Journey: every slot becomes a memory

Job: the payoff Offlyn doesn't have. The slot pill settles into a Journey timeline with upcoming, completed and memory entries, plus photos and the people you met.

Headline

Every slot can lead to something meaningful.

This is the brand promise, word for word.

Reuse

The journey-completed and Journey memory detail screens, and the statistic items ("12 experiences attended · 15 connections").

Ch 09Sun · 2:00 PM

Proof, then a nudge

Job: build trust, then turn some readers into hosts. Counters that tick up, three short quotes, then a band that says "Didn't find it? Host it."

Counters

Experiences hosted · People who showed up · Hosts and professionals · Cities. Use real numbers only.

Band

Didn't find what you're looking for? Someone's waiting for you to host it.

Then

Three cards from Stories: host guides and member journeys.

Ch 10Sun · 9:00 PM

Close: the weekend ends, the point stays

Job: end on the brand's strongest line and the app. The skyline comes back, this time with more windows lit than in the hero.

Copy

Time is the one thing we can never get back. How you spend it makes all the difference.

Actions

Get the app (App Store / Play) · Explore this weekend.

04 · The other pages

One promise per page

Each page follows Offlyn's pattern: a single promise, then the sections that prove it.

Our Story

Manifesto

We aren't here to keep you on your screen. We're here to get you out the door.

Why now: time is getting scarcer→What we believe: the five pillars→Why we built it: founders→Who it's for: Explorers · Hosts · Professionals→What we stand for: human, curious, inclusive, energetic, trustworthy→Close: "Discover more. Connect deeper. Experience life."

The five pillars are already written in the brand story: Human connection, Opportunity for everyone, Real-world discovery, Meaningful time and Community first. A marquee of formats sits between sections: workshops, treks, supper clubs, coaching, open mics, mentorship…

Host a Community

Audience

Bring people together. We'll handle the rest.

Hero + counters→Formats marquee→Your community page→Your host profile→Toolkit: group slots, ticket types, request-to-join, visibility→How to get approved→FAQ

Every section can be built from screens you already have: the Group/community create flow, the Community feed, members and events, and the Community Host application (Identity → Community info → Past experience → Review → Approved). Show them as cards labelled "Example", as Offlyn does.

Share Your Expertise

Audience

Your knowledge, bookable. One slot at a time.

Hero: a session booking mock→Who it's for: coaches, mentors, consultants, teachers→Availability and pricing→Intake form and terms→Credentials and the Verified badge→MySlotMate compared with DMs, payment links and spreadsheets→FAQ

Built from the Professional one-to-one flow (Availability, Pricing, Intake form, Booking settings) and the Credential detail screens.

Explore and its detail pages

Product

Everything happening near you, open to anyone.

/explore→/in/{city}→/e/{experience}→/h/{host}→/c/{community}

This is where the website and the product meet. Web versions of the app screens bring in search traffic. Every page ends with "Continue in the app" for booking.

Stories

Story

Real slots, real people, real weekends.

Member journeys·Host playbooks·This weekend in {city}

This keeps the story going after launch. It also feeds the homepage's Chapter 9 cards and a weekly email.
