We're live. 🎉

Today MySlotMate launches on iOS, Android and the web.

We built it for a simple reason: most of our calendars are full of meetings, and empty of moments. MySlotMate is a marketplace for real-world experiences, like pottery nights, sunrise treks, open mics and supper clubs, hosted by people who love what they do.

If you have a skill, a hobby or a recipe worth sharing, you can host it as a slot. If you've been meaning to "do something this weekend", now you can book it in three taps.
