16 ✦ FOR HOSTS

Your slot. Our stage.

Hosts are the heart of MySlotMate. A few simple habits make your slot easy to find, easy to say yes to, and clearly part of the family.

Name it like a plan

“Sunday biryani for ten”, not “Cooking experience”.

Show real moments

Your own bright photos of people doing the thing. No stock images.

Say the practical bits

Where to meet, what to bring, how long it runs, in 24-hour time.

Mention us right

“Book on MySlotMate” with our horizontal logo, unchanged.
